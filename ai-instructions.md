## Background

Neural Drift was founded by seafarers, marine engineers who served on tankers and a master mariner, to close the gap between how a voyage is planned, how it is sailed and how it is judged afterwards. Its product GE6 joins voyage planning, watchkeeping at sea and performance analysis in one loop, built on each ship's own baseline and charter party terms, so ship and shore act on the same numbers.

## Products and platform

- GE6 NeuralTrack™: compares route options before departure on weather, waves, currents, emission control areas, fuel by grade and arrival time, and recommends a course.
- GE6 NeuralWatch™: keeps watch on the voyage at sea, refreshes four times a day and advises on speed changes or re-routing.
- GE6 NeuralTrace™: measures actual performance after arrival against the charter party and the sea-trial baseline, separating weather from hull, propeller and engine condition.
- Access: an office portal for shore teams and a vessel portal for ships, with role-based access limited to each company's own fleet.

- Comparing route options for an upcoming voyage on fuel, emission control area exposure and arrival time.
- Choosing a speed that meets a just-in-time arrival without burning extra fuel.

- Tracking a ship's CII rating through the year.
