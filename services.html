The platform · SaaS

What's inside

Hull & PerformanceLive

  • Hull Performance Index (0–100) per vessel
  • Six-factor cause decomposition: hull · propeller · engine · weather · operations · unexplained
  • Fuel penalty priced in $/day, with the dominant cause named
  • Fouling rated on the IMO 0–5 scale from in-water evidence
  • Cleaning ROI and drydock projections from repeat inspections
  • Coating performance: actual service life vs the maker's curve

Voyage IntelligenceLive

  • Four route candidates compared on fuel, cost, time — and projected CII band
  • Day-by-day engine-load plan (%MCR) the crew can actually run
  • Fuel-grade switching at emission-control-area boundaries
  • Route files in and out of ECDIS (RTZ import & export)
  • Heavy-weather and adverse-current flags along the track
  • Planned-vs-actual reconciliation feeds each vessel's learning

CII & EmissionsLive

  • Attained vs required CII and the A–E band, computed per MARPOL Annex VI Reg. 28
  • Reference lines and reduction factors held as versioned, dated regulatory data
  • Used up front in voyage planning, not reported after the fact
  • Every figure computed from the noon reports that produced it

Fleet CommandLive

  • Cross-vessel benchmarking — each vessel against its own baseline, not a fleet average
  • Port-state-control risk scored across six regimes
  • Live positions, alerts, and a fleet health view

Ports & ZonesLive

  • 17,520 ports in the reference layer
  • Seven emission-control areas with correct regimes and effective dates
  • ECA crossings computed per planned route
  • Operational port fields stay blank until verified — a dash, never a guess

Bunker IntelligenceRolling out

  • Stem capture with per-grade weighted-average cost
  • Endurance on maximum charter-party consumption, per grade
  • Voyage fuel priced at the date and place of bunkering — never today's market price. That's the basis an auditor accepts.

Onboard AssistantRolling out

  • Ask the vessel in plain language — "why is fuel up this week?"
  • Answers only from your vessel's own data; says "not on file" instead of inventing
  • Paste-a-table noon parsing and bulk import from common voyage-management exports

Built for the seaRolling out

  • Bridge and field surfaces tolerate dropouts — work saves locally and submits on reconnect
  • Role-based access and per-fleet isolation
  • Documents leave the platform tamper-evident and verifiable

Reporting RegimesRoadmap

  • IMO DCS, EU MRV, EU ETS and FuelEU returns generated from the same data spine
  • Year-end CII projection from the vessel's own noons
  • Photo and voice noon capture on the bridge
  • Sequenced to a verifier-acceptable standard before we call any of them live
