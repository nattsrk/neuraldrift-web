How we work

Six rules that shape what we build

  1. 01

    A blank is never a zero

    Where a figure is missing, the screen says so and why. Nothing is estimated in its place.

  2. 02

    Every figure keeps its source

    Each figure shows where it came from, on the screen and in every export.

  3. 03

    The Master decides on board

    We compute and offer. Nothing we show is an instruction to the ship.

  4. 04

    The verifier’s figure counts

    We compute from the data on file. The official figures are the ones your verifier issues.

  5. 05

    Every time carries its zone

    Ship’s time and office time are always shown with their zone. Nothing is left to guess.

  6. 06

    Plain words first

    The sentence a superintendent, a surveyor and a charterer can all follow, then the term of art.
