Who our customers are

The companies that answer for the voyage, the carbon and the hull

GE6 Engine is for the people who own, run, manage and charter merchant ships, and for the crews who sail them. One record per ship serves the owner’s office, the manager’s technical desk, the charterer’s commercial team and the bridge.

Who

Ship owners and operators, ship managers, and charterers.

Which ships

Merchant ships of 5,000 GT and above, the size from which IMO DCS, CII and the EU schemes apply. Bulk, tanker, container, gas and general cargo.

How we start

A pilot on a few ships and the module that pays first. The rest of the fleet follows on the same record.
