The schemes

Five schemes, one record per ship

Our reading at 7 Oct 2026, for planning. Not legal advice; the authority’s current text decides.

SchemeWhat it asksInstrument
EU ETSAllowances for emissions between EU or EEA ports and at berth, and half of those on voyages into or out of them. 2026 is the first year at 100 %.Directive (EU) 2023/959
FuelEU MaritimeA limit on the greenhouse-gas intensity of the energy used on board, tightening every five years.Regulation (EU) 2023/1805
CIIAn A to E rating every year, against a line that tightens to 2030.MARPOL Annex VI, reg. 28
IMO DCSAnnual fuel and distance reporting, with more detail from 1 Jan 2026.MEPC.385(81)
BiofoulingEntry rules that check the hull before a ship is allowed alongside.MEPC.378(80) and national rules

No. We compute from the data on file. The figures that count are the ones your verifier issues.

No. Regulation summaries are our reading for planning. The authority’s current text decides.

Which ships does it cover?

Merchant ships of 5,000 GT and above, the size from which these schemes apply. Biofouling entry rules apply to ships of every size.
