Summaries. The full documents are available on request from fleet@neuraldrifts.com.

Information security

Staff get only the access their work needs. Client data is kept apart by company, and each person sees only their own ships. Traffic is encrypted. If an incident touches a client’s data, we tell that client without undue delay.

Data protection and retention

We collect what we need for the purpose agreed, keep it no longer than that purpose needs, and delete it on request unless the law requires otherwise.

Responsible disclosure

Found a security issue? Write to fleet@neuraldrifts.com with “Security” in the subject. Give us time to fix it before you tell anyone else. We will not act against good-faith research that leaves client data alone.

Use of AI

Where we use AI, it works only with data the person asking is allowed to see. One client’s data is never used to answer another. Models compute and suggest; people decide, and the Master decides on board.

Code of conduct

Honest figures and no claims we cannot support. Respect for the Master’s authority, for every colleague and for every client. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

Anti-bribery and anti-corruption

We do not offer, pay, ask for or accept bribes or facilitation payments, directly or through anyone else. Gifts and hospitality are modest, infrequent and recorded.

Human rights in our supply chain

We do not tolerate forced, bonded or child labour in our business or our supply chain, and we expect the same of our suppliers.

Raising a concern

Anyone can raise a concern in confidence with the directors at fleet@neuraldrifts.com, with “Confidential” in the subject. Nobody will suffer for raising a concern in good faith.
